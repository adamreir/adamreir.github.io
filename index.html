I am a labor economist at the Department of Economics/FAIR at the Norwegian School of Economics, and a research affiliate at RFBerlin.

My research studies how public policies, educational institutions, and information about job characteristics shape labor market outcomes and inequality, with particular attention to inequality across socioeconomic backgrounds and genders.

My work combines Norwegian administrative data, including previously unused records, with text data, surveys, experiments, and large language models.

I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market.

You can read my job market paper, see my CV, and read more about my research.
